Affiliate disclosure

How we earn money, and how we keep that separate from the information we publish.

Draft for approval. This disclosure will be finalised, with partner names, before launch.

HotelsUjjain.in is a hotel discovery guide for Ujjain. We do not take bookings, hold room inventory or process payments. When you decide to stay somewhere, you book with the hotel directly or with a booking partner.

How we make money

Affiliate links

Some links on this site go to booking partners. If you book through one of these links, we may earn a commission from the partner. You do not pay anything extra because of this. Affiliate links are marked as partner links.

Featured listings

Hotels can pay to be featured. Featured listings are always labelled “Featured · Paid”. Payment changes where a listing appears, not the facts we publish about the hotel.

Advertising

Local businesses may advertise on the site. Advertisements are clearly labelled and kept separate from hotel information and guides.

What we never do

  • We never invent hotel names, prices, ratings, amenities, availability or reviews.
  • We never accept payment for reviews or for changing facts about a hotel.
  • We never show a paid placement without a label.

Prices and availability

Prices and availability are set by hotels and booking partners and can change at any time. Always confirm them directly before you book.

Questions

If anything on this page is unclear, please contact us.
