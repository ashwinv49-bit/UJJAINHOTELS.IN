Affiliate disclosure

How we earn money, and how we keep that separate from the information we publish.

Draft for approval. This disclosure will be finalised, with partner names, before launch.

HotelsUjjain.in is a hotel discovery guide for Ujjain. We do not take bookings, hold room inventory or process payments. When you decide to stay somewhere, you book on a booking partner’s website.

How we make money

Affiliate links

Links to booking sites on HotelsUjjain.in are affiliate links. If you book through one of these links, we may earn a commission from the booking site. You do not pay anything extra because of this. Every affiliate link is marked “Partner link”.

No paid listings or advertising

We do not currently accept payment from hotels for listings, and we do not show advertising. If that changes, paid placements and advertisements will always be clearly labelled.

What we never do

  • We never invent hotel names, prices, ratings, amenities, availability or reviews.
  • We never let commissions decide what we write about an area or which booking sites we show.
  • We never show a paid placement without a label.

Prices and availability

Prices and availability are set by hotels and booking sites and can change at any time. Always check them on the booking site before you book.

Questions

If anything on this page is unclear, please contact us.
